# Jumperz

Inspired by true events, Jumperz is the story of a day in the life of one brave frog who did the impossible.

Run, jump, slide, swim, bounce, and tongue-attack your way through mildly inconvenient terrain, underground caverns, the sky, and outer space.

Play the current build here: https://pio687.github.io/Jumperz/jumperz.html

## Learning Disclaimer

This is my second PICO-8 game and I'm still very much learning. Core platformer structure was built following the Nerdy Teachers Platformer Setup playlist (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q6c6DvGK4lg&list=PLyhkEEoUjSQtUiSOu-N4BIrHBFtLNjkyE). 

Child labor used for initial character design and play-testing at critical milestones. Other artwork and sounds, and ideas were generated by my smooth and tiny human brain. Sometimes I use Claude as a "Senior Dev"-style mentor to walk me through debugging and algorithmic iterations, but I'm leaning away from this as I progress.

## Controls

- **Arrow Keys** - Move
- **Z** - Jump/Swim (hold up while swimming to jump out of the water)
- **X** - Super Tongue Attack
