# Jumperz

Inspired by true events, Jumperz is the story of a day in the life of one brave frog who did the impossible.

Run, jump, slide, swim, bounce, and tongue-attack your way through mildly inconvenient terrain, underground caverns, bouncy clouds, outer space, and maybe eventually some cool bonus levels as you attempt to aid - and challenge - the gods themselves.

Behold, Jumperz: https://pio687.github.io/Jumperz/jumperz.html

## Learning Disclaimer

This is my second PICO-8 game and I'm still very much learning. Core platformer structure was built following the Nerdy Teachers Platformer Setup playlist (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q6c6DvGK4lg&list=PLyhkEEoUjSQtUiSOu-N4BIrHBFtLNjkyE). I used Claude as a "Senior Dev"-style mentor for debugging and algorithmic iteration. But AI kind of sucks and doesn't stay in its lane so I used it sparingly. Initial character sprites were produced through unpaid child labor. All other artwork and sounds, and ideas were generated by my smooth and tiny human brain. 

## Controls

- **Arrow Keys** - Move
- **Z** - Jump (spam underwater for a fun surprise)
- **X** - Super Yoshi Tongue Attack
